# Privacy Policy for Clawco Sky

Effective from: [date of publication]

This page says what information we process, why, who can see it, and what you can do. The policy follows GDPR Art. 13 and 14 (verify). It applies to Clawco Sky, not to a Clawco you run yourself.

## 1. Who is responsible

Henrik Lambert (a private individual, no company), company no. no CVR number (not VAT-registered; registers when the legal threshold is reached), [Adresse], [Kontakt-e-mail] is the controller of your account information and payment status. Data protection officer: [DPO or "not appointed"].

Your content in the instance is a special case, see section 3.

## 2. What we process about you

**Account data.** Your name, your email address, the public part of your passkey, your subscription and your payment status. Card details are held by Stripe, not by us. If you are in a household, the other members can see your name.

**Technical data.** When you sign in and use the service, our systems see for example your IP address and timestamps. We use them for operations and security. We keep them for [number] days. Error reports are scrubbed of content: they never contain your messages, email text or prompts.

**Usage patterns, only if you say yes.** If you switch it on, your instance sends anonymous figures about how the product is used, for example which connections are added and which buttons are used. These are shapes, not content, and cannot be traced back to you. You can switch it off again at any time.

**Emails from us.** We send the emails you need: sign-in links, receipts, reminders about the trial and renewal, and important changes. We send newsletters and offers only if you have said yes.

## 3. Your content in your instance

You can connect your email, calendar, notes and more to Clawco. It is your content. We process it only to provide the service to you, inside your own isolated instance. In addition:

- Our staff do not read it. Support can only get in if you yourself switch on "Lad support se med" ("let support look along"). It is off by default, time-limited ([duration]), and everything support does is logged.
- We do not sell it.
- We do not use it to train models, neither ours nor anyone else's.
- We do not pass it on, beyond what section 5 says.

The split of roles is: you decide what you connect, and we are the processor for the content. If you want a data processing agreement, you can have one. [Lawyer: clarify the roles. If the customer is a private person, the household exemption in GDPR Art. 2(2)(c) may apply to the customer but not to us — verify.]

Your instance sends the parts of your content that the model needs to read to the model provider you have chosen with your own key. That happens under your agreement with the provider.

## 4. Why we may process the information

- **Contract** (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): account, subscription, running your instance and payment.
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f)): security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. We weigh it against your rights. [The assessment must be documented.]
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c)): bookkeeping, see the Danish Bookkeeping Act (verify).
- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)): anonymous usage patterns and marketing emails. You can withdraw consent without affecting what happened before.

All article references in this section: verify.

## 5. Who receives the information

We use these subprocessors (the full list is in Subprocessors):

- Hetzner Online GmbH: hosting of instances and backups, data centres in the EU
- Stripe Payments Europe Ltd: payment, subscription and VAT
- [E-mailudbyder]: emails from us, EU provider
- [Fejlovervågning]: error reports without content
- [Modeludbydere]: only if you use the "included model" add-on

We only pass information to public authorities if the law requires it.

## 6. Transfers outside the EU

We store your content and account data in the EU. Some of our suppliers have parent companies or subcontractors outside the EU, for example in the USA. If a transfer takes place, it rests on a valid basis, for example the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) or an equivalent decision (GDPR Chapter V — verify). [Verify per supplier, especially Stripe, [Fejlovervågning] and [Modeludbydere].]

## 7. How long we keep it

- Account data: while the account exists and for 30 days after it is deleted.
- Your content: until you delete the account. Then the instance stops and the data is deleted, see Deletion and export.
- Backups: at most 30 days. Deleted content disappears from backups within 30 days.
- Accounting records (invoices): 5 years after the end of the financial year (Bookkeeping Act — verify).
- Technical logs: [number] days.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** what we hold about you (Art. 15)
- **rectification** of wrong information (Art. 16)
- **erasure** (Art. 17), which you can do yourself in your account
- **restriction** of processing (Art. 18)
- **data portability** (Art. 20): you can download everything as a zip file
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
- **withdraw consent**

Write to [Kontakt-e-mail]. We answer within one month. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk). All article references: verify.

## 9. Children

Clawco Sky is for people aged 13 and over. A child can be a member of a household, but only if a parent or another person with parental responsibility consents on the child's behalf (Data Protection Act section 6, GDPR Art. 8 — verify). The customer confirms the consent when the child is added. The parent can withdraw it at any time and have the child's data deleted. A child under 13 cannot create an account alone.

## 10. Automated decisions

We make no decisions with legal effect on you based solely on automated processing (GDPR Art. 22 — verify). The agent's suggestions are always confirmed by a human before anything happens. And the agent never pays, writes to others or deletes without your yes.

## 11. Changes and contact

We announce material changes by email at least 30 days ahead. Questions: [Kontakt-e-mail], [Adresse]. Data protection officer: [DPO or "not appointed"].
