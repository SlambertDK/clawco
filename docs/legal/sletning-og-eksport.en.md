# Deletion and Export in Clawco Sky

Effective from: [date of publication]

Your content is yours. You can take it with you, and you can delete it. You do not have to ask us for permission, and you do not have to write to us. This page says how, and what happens afterwards.

## Export: take everything with you

You can export at any time while your instance is running.

[Menu names and buttons must be checked against the product.]

1. Go to Settings in your instance.
2. Choose "Eksportér alt" (export everything).
3. You get a zip file with your content: your notes, your rules, your settings and your logs.

The zip file holds the same kind of folders that Clawco uses when you run it yourself. So you can move straight to Clawco at home or in your own cloud. It is free, and it is under the AGPL-3.0 licence. What you put in yourself comes with it. Your model keys are encrypted and are not included in the zip file, so you must enter them again [verify against the product]. You get your invoices from the payment portal.

Export costs nothing and is not limited in number. If the file is very large, it may take a few minutes before it is ready.

## Deletion: delete your account yourself

You can delete your account in Settings. We ask for your passkey or a link by email, so that no one deletes your things by mistake. You are asked whether you want to export first.

In a household, only the owner can delete the whole household. A member can delete their own account. A child member can be deleted by a parent.

## What happens, and in which order

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | You confirm the deletion. Your instance is stopped at once. No one can sign in. Your model keys are revoked. We take no more backups. |
| Day 0 to 7 | Your instance and its volume are wiped and overwritten. Your account data is marked for deletion. [Proposal: at the latest 7 days.] |
| Within 30 days | Your backups expire. After day 30 your content no longer exists with us. Your account data is deleted the same day. |
| 5 years | We keep accounting records (invoices and payments) because the law requires it. The Danish Bookkeeping Act requires 5 years after the end of the financial year (verify). They hold only what is needed: name, email, amount and date. None of your content. |

A deletion cannot be undone. We use backups for operations, not to restore an account you have deleted. [Proposal.]

## When the subscription ends

You do not have to delete. If you cancel, your instance runs to the end of the period you have paid for. After that it stops. You have 30 days to take out your data. If you do nothing, it is deleted after the 30 days, and then the timeline above follows from day 0.

The same applies if the free 14-day trial ends without a subscription, or if a payment fails and is not put right. We write to you before we delete.

## The timeline in days

- Export: at any time while the instance runs, and for 30 days after it ends.
- Instance stopped: day 0.
- Instance and volume wiped: at the latest day 7.
- Backups expired and account data deleted: at the latest day 30 after the deletion.
- Accounting records: 5 years after the end of the financial year.

## Your rights

You have the right to erasure under GDPR Art. 17 and to move your data under Art. 20 (verify both). Write to [Kontakt-e-mail] if you cannot get started. We confirm the deletion in writing if you ask. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.
