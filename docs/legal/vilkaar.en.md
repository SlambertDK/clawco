Clawco Sky is for private use. You must be at least 18 to create an account and buy a subscription. [Proposal, verify.]

## 2. What Clawco Sky is

Clawco Sky is Clawco, hosted by us in the EU. Each customer gets their own isolated instance. No one shares an instance, and no instance has a public port. The product inside the instance is the same as the one you can run yourself.

You can also run Clawco yourself, at home or in your own cloud. That is free and is covered by the AGPL-3.0 licence, not by these terms. These terms apply only to the service we host.

Clawco reads, sorts and suggests. Answers and suggestions from a language model can be wrong. You decide what happens.

## 3. Account and household

You sign in with a passkey or a link sent by email. There are no passwords. You must look after your email and your devices. Tell us at once if anyone else can get in.

With the Sky Husstand plan you can invite up to six people to your household. Each person gets their own account. You may only invite people who have agreed to it.

Children can be members of the household. A parent or another person with parental responsibility must consent on the child's behalf, and you, as the customer, confirm this when you add the child. Under 13, the law requires the parent's consent (Danish Data Protection Act section 6, GDPR Art. 8 — verify). A child cannot create an account alone before turning 13.

## 4. How you may use the service

You may not:

- use the service for anything unlawful
- attack, overload or try to break into the service or other customers' instances
- share your account with people outside your household or resell access
- use the service for business purposes
- use unreasonably many resources for your plan [fair-use limits: to be defined]

If you break the rules, we can stop your instance. We write to you first, unless it is urgent for security reasons. You can always take your data out, see Deletion and export.

## 5. The three limits: our promises

Clawco has three limits, and they also apply in Clawco Sky:

1. The agent never pays for anything.
2. The agent never writes to other people. It does not send email or messages on your behalf.
3. The agent never deletes anything without your explicit yes, every time.

The limits are built into the code itself, not only into instructions to the model. The code stops an action even if the model proposes it. Clawco Sky offers no way to pay, send or delete on your behalf. Requests between members of the same household inside Clawco do not count as messages to others. [Product note: check against the household "go-between" before publishing.]

The limits do not mean that suggestions are correct. You must read what you say yes to.

## 6. Models

**Your own key.** By default you use your own key for a language model. You pay the provider directly and you are responsible for the provider's terms and prices, including whether your subscription with the provider may be used this way. Your key is stored encrypted in your own instance. Your instance sends the parts of your content that the model needs to read to the provider you chose. That happens under your agreement with the provider, not ours.

**Included model.** Later you can add "included model". You then get a monthly amount of model use through our own keys, with a fixed cap. When the cap is reached, the model stops, and we charge nothing more without your yes. Unused amounts do not carry over. [Proposal.] The add-on has its own price, which you see before you buy. In that case the model providers are subprocessors, see the privacy policy.

## 7. Subscription and prices

You can choose Sky Solo (one person) or Sky Husstand (up to six people). Prices are on the pricing page and are shown again at checkout, including VAT, before you pay. You pay month by month or year by year. Payment goes through Stripe. We do not store your card details.

## 8. Free trial

You can try for 14 days without a card. When the 14 days are over, your instance stops unless you have chosen a plan. You then have 30 days to take out your data, and after that it is deleted.

## 9. Renewal and cancellation

The subscription renews automatically for the same period, at the same price, until you cancel. For yearly subscriptions we send an email reminder before renewal. [Proposal: 14 days before.]

You can cancel whenever you like with one click in your account. It is as easy as signing up. The subscription runs to the end of the period you have paid for. You get no refund for a period already begun, unless the law or these terms give you the right. [Refund policy: to be decided.]

If a payment fails, Stripe tries again. If that does not help, we stop your instance and write to you. You have 30 days to pay or take out your data.

## 10. Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal from the day you buy a paid subscription (Danish Consumer Contracts Act section 17 — verify). Write to [Kontakt-e-mail] to withdraw.

You can ask us to start right away. When you buy, you therefore confirm:

- that you expressly ask us to start the service immediately
- that you know you lose the right of withdrawal once the service has been fully provided (Consumer Contracts Act section 18(2) no. 13 — verify)
- that, if you withdraw after we have started, you pay for what we have provided until then (Consumer Contracts Act section 20 — verify)

We take a backup every night and keep it for 30 days. A backup is a safety net, not an archive. Take your own data out when it matters.
