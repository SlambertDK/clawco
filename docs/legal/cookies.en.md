# Cookies on clawco.work and Clawco Sky

Effective from: [date of publication]

In short: clawco.work sets no cookies. When you sign in, we use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. We do not track you. That is why the site has no cookie banner.

## What we set

| Cookie | What it does | When | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Host-sky` | Remembers that you are signed in to your account (session) | When you sign in at konto.clawco.work | 30 days, or until you sign out |
| `__Host-sky-binding` | Ties a sign-in link to the browser you asked for it in | When you ask for a sign-in link | 15 minutes |
| `__Host-sky-til` | Remembers which page to take you back to after sign-in | When you sign in from a particular page | 15 minutes |
| `__Host-clawco` | Remembers that you are signed in to your own Clawco | When you open your Clawco | 30 days, or until you sign out. The code in it must be renewed every 15 minutes. |

That is all of them. They are necessary for you to sign in and use the service, and they are never used for anything else.

## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that your browser stores when you visit a site. The cookie on your account holds a random code. The cookie on your Clawco holds a short-lived, signed code with your account number and your email address, so your Clawco knows who is signed in. Neither holds your content. They only tell us and our gateway that you are the one who is signed in, so you do not have to sign in again at every click.

## Two addresses, same rule

The cookies are set only on the addresses where you sign in: konto.clawco.work and the address of your own Clawco, which is also under clawco.work. clawco.work itself sets none. Your household members get their own cookies in their own browser. Each cookie belongs only to the exact address that set it, is sent only over HTTPS (Secure), cannot be read by scripts on the page (HttpOnly) and is not sent along when another site fetches something from us in the background (SameSite=Lax).

## What we do not do

- We use no cookies for statistics, advertising or tracking.
- We use no third-party analytics tools on the site.
- We do not let others set cookies through our pages.
- We do not sell information about how you use the site.

We collect no usage statistics without cookies either, see the Privacy Policy.

## Why there is no banner

Danish rules require consent for cookies that are not necessary (the Cookie Order, Cookiebekendtgørelsen — verify). The cookies above are necessary for the service you ask for yourself, and do not need consent. If we have no other cookies, there is nothing to ask about. We inform you here instead.

If we ever want to use more cookies, we will first change this page and ask for your consent.

## If you block the cookies

If you block the cookies, you cannot be signed in, because the service cannot remember you from one page to the next. The public part of the site, such as the pricing page and the terms, still works. There is no other way to sign in, because we use no passwords or tracking to recognise you.

## When you pay

Payment takes place on a page that Stripe owns. Stripe sets its own cookies there, and they are Stripe's responsibility. See Stripe's own policy. We do not see Stripe's cookies.

## Fonts and other external resources

clawco.work and the account pages at konto.clawco.work load fonts and other files from our own addresses, not from third parties. The Clawco app in your instance loads the fonts Inter and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts. Google therefore receives your IP address when the app loads. Google Fonts sets no cookies.

## How to delete the cookies

You can sign out in the service. Then they stop working. You can also delete them yourself:

- **Chrome:** Settings, Privacy and security, Delete browsing data, choose Cookies.
- **Safari:** Settings, Privacy, Manage Website Data, find clawco.work and choose Remove.
- **Firefox:** Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data, Manage Data.
- **Edge:** Settings, Cookies and site permissions, Manage and delete cookies.

When you delete them, you must sign in again with your passkey or your sign-in link. Menu names may differ slightly between versions.

## Changes

We write changes here and update the version line at the top. If the change is material, we tell you in the service before it takes effect.
