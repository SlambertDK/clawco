# Cookies on clawco.work and Clawco Sky

Effective from: [date of publication]

In short: we set one cookie, and only if you are signed in. We do not track you. That is why the site has no cookie banner.

| Cookie | What it does | When | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| [cookie name] | Remembers that you are signed in (session) | When you sign in | [duration] or until you sign out |

That is the only one. It is necessary for you to use the service, and it is never used for anything else.

## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that your browser stores when you visit a site. The session cookie holds a random code, not your name or your content. The code only tells our gateway that you are the one who is signed in, so you do not have to sign in again at every click.

## Two addresses, same rule

The cookie is set on the addresses where you are signed in: on clawco.work and on the address of your own instance ([instance address, for example your-name.clawco.app]). Your household members get their own cookie in their own browser. The cookie cannot be read by other sites. [Verify against the code: secure cookie flags such as Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite.]

## What we do not do

- We use no cookies for statistics, advertising or tracking.
- We use no third-party analytics tools on the site.
- We do not let others set cookies through our pages.
- We do not sell information about how you use the site.

If you switch on "anonymous usage patterns" in your instance, that does not use cookies. It is a choice you make yourself, and it is described in the Privacy Policy.

## Why there is no banner

Danish rules require consent for cookies that are not necessary (the Cookie Order, Cookiebekendtgørelsen — verify). The session cookie is necessary for the service you ask for yourself, and does not need consent. If we have no other cookies, there is nothing to ask about. We inform you here instead.

If we ever want to use more cookies, we will first change this page and ask for your consent.

## If you block the cookie

If you block the cookie, you cannot be signed in, because the service cannot remember you from one page to the next. The public part of the site, such as the pricing page and the terms, still works. There is no other way to sign in, because we use no passwords or tracking to recognise you.

## When you pay

Payment takes place on a page that Stripe owns. Stripe sets its own cookies there, and they are Stripe's responsibility. See Stripe's own policy. We do not see Stripe's cookies.

## How to delete the cookie

You can sign out in the service. Then it disappears. You can also delete it yourself:

- **Chrome:** Settings, Privacy and security, Delete browsing data, choose Cookies.
- **Safari:** Settings, Privacy, Manage Website Data, find clawco.work and choose Remove.
- **Firefox:** Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data, Manage Data.
- **Edge:** Settings, Cookies and site permissions, Manage and delete cookies.

When you delete it, you must sign in again with your passkey or your sign-in link. Menu names may differ slightly between versions.
