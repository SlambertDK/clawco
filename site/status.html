                How this page works

                This page is served from a different place than Clawco Sky, so a fault in Sky cannot take it down.

                Your browser loads one small file from konto.clawco.work, Clawco's own account server. If that file is missing, older than 5 minutes or unreadable, every part shows as unknown and the banner says we cannot measure. Silence is never shown as working.

                Nothing about individual customers is published, only the share of customers' Clawco that answers, as a percentage. The page sets no cookies, loads nothing from third parties and counts nothing.
