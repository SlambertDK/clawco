Connect a first source

  1. On your account page, open the address next to “Din Clawco:”. While it is being made, the page says it takes a few minutes.
  2. Start is the first room. It walks you through a few steps, one card each.
  3. Under “Vælg din model”, paste a key from your own provider into Start, and nowhere else.
  4. Under “Første kilde”, choose a calendar or your mail and follow the card. Right after, something to look at stands in Nu.

More sources come one at a time, and only if you want them. Each new source gives something to look at straight away.

Clawco reads from a source. What it may do with what it reads is in the security promise.
