Sign in on a new device

A passkey lives on the phone or computer where you made it. A new device does not have one yet, so Clawco sends a sign-in link to your mail instead. There are no passwords.

  1. Open the sign-in page of your account.
  2. Type your email address and press “Send mig et link”.
  3. Open the mail in the same browser you asked from. The link works once, for 15 minutes, and only in that browser.
  4. Press “Log ind”.
  5. To skip the mail next time, press “Tilføj en passkey” on your account page. Adding one needs a sign-in from the last 5 minutes.

No mail after a few minutes? Check the spam folder, then ask again. The page answers the same whether or not the address has an account, on purpose.
