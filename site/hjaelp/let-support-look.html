Can support look at my Clawco?

No. There is no “Lad support se med” switch in this version. Nobody at Clawco can open your Clawco from our side, and there is no support tool that gives us a way in.

We only see what you send us. Whoever runs the server has technical access to its disks. That access is used to run the service and never to read your content. The privacy policy says the same.

A switch that lets you allow a look for a limited time, with every look logged, may come later. It does not exist now, and nothing you do today turns it on.

How to get help instead

Press “Få hjælp” in your account, or write to the address below. Describe what you see, or paste the text of an error. Send only what you choose to send.
