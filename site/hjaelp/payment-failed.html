A payment failed

Stripe tells Clawco when a payment fails. Your subscription page then reads “Betalingen mangler siden” and the date.

  1. On your account page, press “Se abonnement og planer”.
  2. Press “Kort, fakturaer og planskift”. Stripe’s page for your subscription opens. The card is entered there and never in Clawco.

What is not touched

  • Nothing is deleted because a payment failed.
  • In this version a failed payment does not stop or lock your Clawco. The rules for a payment that stays overdue are not switched on yet.
