Delete my account

  1. On your account page, press “Slet min konto”. The page lists what ends and offers “Hent alt først”. If you have not signed in within the last 5 minutes, you are asked to sign in again.
  2. Type your email address under “Skriv din mail for at bekræfte” and press “Slet min konto for altid”.

If you own a Clawco, deleting your account deletes it and everything in it, and the people you invited lose access. A household member deletes only their own account; the household goes on for the others.

There is no grace period and no undo. Your subscription is cancelled with the account.

What is kept

  • Backups of your Clawco disappear within 30 days.
  • Invoices are kept for 5 years, because the Danish Bookkeeping Act requires it.
  • A receipt that the deletion happened, with nothing from your account in it.
