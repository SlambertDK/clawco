Household

A household is the people who share one Clawco. Each person keeps their own Nu, their own notes and their own private and work data. The household shares one week and a few things.

The room: Husstanden

It opens when two or more people are in the household, or when a shared thing or a request exists. For one person alone it is not there.

Who is in it

A member is a person on your own Tailscale network, with a login, a name and a role. The owner adds a person from the invite page, which opens when the owner asks the chat to invite someone.

A household holds up to 6 people.

An own entry: Clawco shows the owner the steps for sharing the machine with that person in Tailscale's admin, one at a time. Clawco never changes Tailscale for the owner and makes no account or password of its own. The person is activated on their first visit.

Phone only: a person with no entry of their own is reached through the go-between, described below.

Tailscale

Tailscale's free Personal plan allows up to 6 users and unlimited devices, and is meant for non-commercial use. Checked on 2026-11-02 on Tailscale's plans page.

Tailscale's plans

Roles

  • ejer Reads and acts, changes the settings and the household, stops everything and starts it again.
  • medlem Reads and acts, sees the shared week and the shared things, and can stop everything.
  • barn The same as a member.
  • gæst Reads only, and sees what is addressed to them.

The server checks the role on every action. A button that is hidden is not the protection.

Zones

Everything Clawco knows sits in one of three zones. No role ever sees another person's private or work data. A calendar is read into the shared week only when it is in the household zone.

  • privat Your own, private.
  • arbejde Your own, for work.
  • husstand Shared by the household.

Forespørgsel a request between two members

One member asks another for something, for example to take a child on Thursday. Both see its state. Only a person's tap moves it on; the agent may at most suggest an answer.

  • spurgt asked
  • accepteret accepted
  • afvist declined
  • alternativ another time or answer proposed
  • udloebet expired without an answer

Ressource a shared thing with owners and a calendar

An owner books it with one tap; anyone else asks an owner. When two bookings overlap, it becomes one decision for the two who booked, never a decision the agent makes.

  • bil a car
  • rum a room
  • ting a thing
  • person a person

The weekend check

On Tuesdays Clawco looks at the coming weekend. When two adults are busy at the same time and something has to be covered, both get the same neutral card. A pick only becomes a request to the other person; nothing is decided for them.

The go-between

A person with no entry of their own is reached on a channel your Hermes already speaks: Telegram, WhatsApp or Signal. A request goes out as a message only when the owner taps send, and the text sent is the text shown. Nothing is sent before their first yes except the invitation, and nothing after they say stop.

Their reply counts only when it is exactly yes, no or a time, from their own number in a direct chat. Anything else is shown as text and never acts. They install nothing.

The go-between needs Hermes with a messaging gateway on the same machine. It is tested against a scripted channel.
