Write to us

kontakt@clawco.work

We answer within 2 working days.

In your account, “Få hjælp” opens a mail to this address with a few technical facts already in it: your account number (not your email), your plan and its state, the versions, where your Clawco runs and, if there is one, the last error code. You read and edit it before you send. Nothing from inside your Clawco is ever added.

The account is in Danish for now, so its buttons are named here as they appear there.

Never send us your model key, a password, a passkey or card details. We do not ask for them, and nothing in this help flow collects them.
